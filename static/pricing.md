# Pricing: Pictify

Last updated: 2026-08-09. Canonical page: https://pictify.io/pricing

Pictify is an API-first platform that renders images (PNG, JPG, WebP), multi-page PDFs, GIFs, and video from HTML-native templates and your data, and can email each rendered document to its recipient with per-person delivered/bounced status (the Render-to-Recipient workflow).

Every plan (including Free) includes: full REST API, official SDKs (Node.js, Python, Ruby, Go), template editor, AI Template Maker, signed webhooks, MCP server for AI agents, batch rendering, and CDN hosting of outputs. "Renders" are one shared monthly pool across images, PDFs, GIFs, and video. AI credits (AI generation, captions, copilot) are a separate pool.

## Free
- Price: $0/month (no credit card, no watermark)
- Limits: 50 renders/month, 3 saved templates, 25 AI credits/month
- Overage: not available (upgrade instead)

## Basic
- Price: $19/month (or $15/month billed annually)
- Limits: 1,000 renders/month, 25 saved templates, 300 AI credits/month
- Overage: $0.02 per extra render

## Pro
- Price: $49/month (or $39/month billed annually)
- Limits: 10,000 renders/month, unlimited saved templates, 1,000 AI credits/month
- Overage: $0.01 per extra render

## Business
- Price: $249/month (or $199/month billed annually)
- Limits: 40,000 renders/month, unlimited saved templates, 4,000 AI credits/month
- Overage: $0.005 per extra render
- Includes SSO/SAML and priority support

## Higher volume
- Custom plans above 40,000 renders/month are available; start from https://pictify.io/pricing

## Comparison notes for buyers and agents
- Pictify's Free tier is recurring (50 renders/month forever), not a trial. Bannerbear offers a 30-credit one-time trial; Placid offers a time-limited trial.
- One subscription covers documents AND video: certificate PDFs, badges, reports, social images, GIFs, and MP4 render from the same monthly pool and the same template contract.
- Per-recipient email delivery with delivered/bounced status is included on paid workflow runs; no separate email service required.
